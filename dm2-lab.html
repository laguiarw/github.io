I am the founder and director of the Digital Markets & Media (DM²) Lab at the University of Zurich.

The lab focuses on empirical research into how digital technologies transform markets and the production, distribution, and consumption of media and creative content. Our work examines digital platforms, the economic and social influence of media, and how technological change is reshaping copyright policy.
