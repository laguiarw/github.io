Luis Aguiar

Associate Professor
Management & Economics of Digital Transformation
University of Zurich

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Business Administration and a DSI Professor with the Digital Society Initiative at the University of Zurich. I am also a Research Associate at ZEW Mannheim and a Research Fellow of the CESifo Research Network.

I am an empirical economist focusing on two interconnected areas of today's digital transformation. My first broad line of research examines how digitization affects markets, consumers, and firms, with a particular focus on the media and content industries. Second, I study the economics of intellectual property, exploring how technological change affects the economic foundations of copyright policy. Across both areas, I aim to generate insights that inform policy design and managerial decision-making.

I currently serve as co-editor-in-chief for Information Economics and Policy.

I hold an ERC Consolidator Grant for the project "Rethinking Copyright Policy in the Digital Age: Balancing Incentives for Creation and Access" (RECODE), 2026-2031. Learn more about the project here.

I am the PI of the project "Digitization and the Power of Online Platforms: Measurement and Implications" financed by the Swiss National Science Foundation, 2022-2026.
